# «Он идёт за вами» — часть 1. Промпты для Google Flow

Реалистичная ночная съёмка, как снято на камеру: панельный двор, мокрый асфальт, оранжевые фонари,
морось. Почти всё — от первого лица («вы») или со спины, поэтому главный герой не нужен.
Мужчина — всегда без лица: глубокий капюшон, внутри только чёрная тень.

Flow: **Ingredients to Video**, 9:16, **Veo Fast** (или Lite, если есть — дешевле). 12 клипов.

## Шаг 1. Ингредиенты (режим «Изображение», 9:16)

**МУЖЧИНА**
```
A tall broad-shouldered man in a dark oversized hooded parka standing under a single streetlight at night in an empty Russian residential courtyard. The hood is deep and his face is completely hidden in pitch-black shadow — no eyes, no facial features visible at all, only darkness inside the hood. Hands in pockets, motionless. Photorealistic cinematic night photo, orange sodium streetlight behind him, wet asphalt with reflections, light drizzle, cold blue shadows, film grain. Vertical 9:16.
```

**ДВОР**
```
Empty courtyard of Russian nine-story concrete panel apartment blocks at night, a building entrance door with a metal intercom panel and a dim lamp above it, one orange sodium streetlight, old parked cars, wet asphalt with puddles, bare trees, a few lit windows, light drizzle. Photorealistic cinematic night photo, cold blue shadows, film grain, no people. Vertical 9:16.
```

## Шаг 2. Клипы

Хвост стиля уже в каждом промпте. В скобках — какие ингредиенты добавить.

**01** (МУЖЧИНА + ДВОР)
```
Wide static shot of the empty night courtyard. Far away under the streetlight stands the tall man in the dark hooded parka, perfectly still, his face invisible in black shadow, light rain falling through the lamp light. Slow creeping push-in. No one speaks. Photorealistic cinematic night footage, orange sodium light, wet asphalt, cold blue shadows, film grain, vertical 9:16, no text.
```

**02** (ДВОР)
```
First-person POV walking at night from a bus stop into a residential courtyard of panel blocks, handheld camera bobbing with each step, empty wet street, orange streetlights, puddles reflecting light, breath vapor in front of the camera. No one speaks. Photorealistic cinematic night footage, cold blue shadows, film grain, vertical 9:16, no text.
```

**03** (МУЖЧИНА + ДВОР)
```
First-person POV: the camera turns to look back over the shoulder while walking. About twenty meters behind, the tall man in the dark hooded parka walks at the same pace, backlit by a streetlight, his face a black void inside the hood. Handheld, tense. No one speaks. Photorealistic cinematic night footage, orange sodium light, wet asphalt, cold blue shadows, film grain, vertical 9:16, no text.
```

**04** (МУЖЧИНА)
```
Low angle close-up on wet asphalt: a pair of white sneakers walking fast toward the camera and past it; a few seconds later heavy black boots step into the same puddle, keeping the same rhythm. Splashes, orange reflections. No one speaks. Photorealistic cinematic night footage, cold blue shadows, film grain, vertical 9:16, no text.
```

**05** (ДВОР)
```
Close-up of trembling hands searching inside a shoulder bag for keys at night, under a streetlight, breath vapor drifting, light rain on the bag. Then the hands freeze as if listening. No one speaks. Photorealistic cinematic night footage, orange sodium light, cold blue shadows, film grain, vertical 9:16, no text.
```

**06** (МУЖЧИНА)
```
Medium shot: the tall man in the dark hooded parka stands still under a streetlight and looks down at his phone. The phone screen lights from below, but inside the hood there is still only pitch-black emptiness, no face. Rain falls through the light. Slow push-in. No one speaks. Photorealistic cinematic night footage, cold blue shadows, film grain, vertical 9:16, no text.
```

**07** (ДВОР)
```
First-person POV walking along a panel apartment block at night toward a building entrance with a metal intercom panel and a lamp above the door, then continuing past the entrance without stopping, the door sliding out of frame. Handheld. No one speaks. Photorealistic cinematic night footage, orange sodium light, wet asphalt, film grain, vertical 9:16, no text.
```

**08** (МУЖЧИНА + ДВОР)
```
Shot from behind a parked car at night: in the distance the tall man in the dark hooded parka stops, turns and walks straight to the building entrance with the intercom, his face hidden in shadow. Rain, puddles. No one speaks. Photorealistic cinematic night footage, orange sodium light, cold blue shadows, film grain, vertical 9:16, no text.
```

**09** (МУЖЧИНА)
```
Extreme close-up of a metal intercom keypad at a building entrance at night: a finger in a black glove slowly presses button 4, then button 7. The small display glows green. Raindrops on the panel. No one speaks. Photorealistic cinematic night footage, film grain, vertical 9:16.
```

**10** (ДВОР)
```
Extreme close-up of the intercom speaker grille and the glowing display at night, rain drops on the metal, the display blinking as it calls. Very slow push-in. No one speaks. Photorealistic cinematic night footage, cold blue and green light, film grain, vertical 9:16, no text.
```

**11** (ДВОР)
```
Exterior of a nine-story panel apartment block at night, slow tilt up along the windows; all windows are dark, then one window on the fourth floor suddenly turns on with warm light. Rain. No one speaks. Photorealistic cinematic night footage, orange sodium light, cold blue shadows, film grain, vertical 9:16, no text.
```

**12** (МУЖЧИНА + ДВОР)
```
The tall man in the dark hooded parka stands at the building entrance, slowly turns his head toward the camera; inside the hood only darkness. Rain falls through the lamp light above the door. Hold still, then a slight handheld shake. No one speaks. Photorealistic cinematic night footage, orange sodium light, cold blue shadows, film grain, vertical 9:16, no text.
```
